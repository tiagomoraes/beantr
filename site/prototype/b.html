Install

Every bean, every brew, remembered.

Beantr gives the assistant you already use a memory for your coffee: bags, gear, recipes and every cup you brew.

curl -fsSL https://beantr.tiagomoraes.cloud/install | bash -s -- all

One command. It installs for every assistant it finds on this machine, and keeps your notes at ~/beantr.

Why it feels different.

  1. 1

    It lives inside your agent.

    No new app to open, no account, no dashboard. You talk about coffee the way you already talk to it about everything else.

  2. 2

    Your notes are plain files.

    Markdown, in a folder on your machine. Open them in any editor, sync them with anything, take them with you if you ever leave.

  3. 3

    It never guesses.

    A fact it does not know is written down as unknown. Every recommendation points to cups you actually brewed.

  4. 4

    Today's shelf is always clear.

    What is open, what is resting, what to finish first. No digging through old entries to find out.

  5. 5

    Nothing is ever lost.

    Every brew and every bag stays in the log. Entries are added, never edited away.
