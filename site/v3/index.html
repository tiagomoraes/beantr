README.md

why it is different

Why it feels different.

It lives inside your agent.

No new app to open, no account, no dashboard. You talk about coffee the way you already talk to it about everything else.

Your notes are plain files.

Markdown in a folder on your disk. Open it in any editor, sync it with anything, take it with you.

It never guesses.

A fact it does not know is written as unknown. Every recommendation points to a cup you actually brewed.

One coffee per container.

A named jar or tube holds one coffee at a time. Your agent asks before it replaces anything still there.

Nothing is ever lost.

Every brew and every bag stays in the log. Entries are added, never edited away.
